Organization of the course
The course will consist of ‘live’ on-campus lectures, a few online lectures, Q&A sessions, work sessions and a small-group assignment.  Lectures will be recorded for later review via Brightspace. Work sessions and group assignments will include digital and online bioinformatics tasks. On-campus activities will take place in several different locations in the Van Steenis Building (STE) of Gorlaeus building (GOR). The mini-symposium at the end of the course will take place in the Atrium of the Gorlaeus research building. For exact timing and location please follow the schedule below, as well as regularly consult MyTimeTable. 

Daily course blocks are indicative: Start times of blocks are fixed, sub- and end-timing is indicative. Any changes in timing and/or location will be communicated via announcements in Brightspace. 

The full schedule of the course, including additional information, program changes and additions is posted and updated daily in Brightspace.

Week 4 – Group Project and Final Exam

Monday 16 Nov. – Group research and retrieve project
The purpose of this project week is to prepare a poster presentation with a group of (max.) 6 students, in which you summarize what is known about the genomic architecture of your species of choice, and what makes your species so special in the context of this course.
With a working group of (max.) 6 students, select a research topic from the list that will be posted on Brightspace. Make sure to list a first, second and third choice. Email your subject of choice to coordinator Han de Winde (j.h.de.winde@biology.leidenuniv.nl).

More details to be given a.s.a.p.
Note: in this week you are free to do your own research and retrieval, and to study for the final exam; no further lectures are planned.
